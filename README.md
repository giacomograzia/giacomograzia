#### 📄 Publications

- **Mixture of Experts for Fine-Grained Shape Refinement in Medical Segmentation**<br>
  MICCAI Shape in Medical Imaging Workshop, 2026 · [paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=64REK28ZDA)<br>
  A mixture-of-experts refiner for small pelvic-fracture fragments in X-rays, evaluated on 26,000+
  fragments. It reduces boundary error by over 65% versus a strong baseline.

- **Reproducing "The Tug of War Within: Mitigating the Fairness–Privacy Conflicts in Large Language Models"**<br>
  *Transactions on Machine Learning Research* (TMLR), 2026 · [paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=7nKvpj17oI) · [code](https://github.com/giacomograzia/reproducing-SPIN)<br>
  Reproduces and extends SPIN across three instruction-tuned 7B LLMs: up to 6.1 percentage points better
  fairness–privacy trade-offs with at most a 0.6-point drop in general capability, plus new experiments on
  malicious data, limited data and safety–helpfulness conflicts.
